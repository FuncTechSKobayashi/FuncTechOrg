MapTech (iPhone and iPad App) Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 / Last updated: November 13, 2026

FuncTech K.K. ("we," "us," or "our") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how we handle user information in "MapTech," the sales support map app for iPhone and iPad that we provide (the "App").

This Policy applies to the iPhone and iPad version of MapTech. It does not apply to "MapTech," our map app for Salesforce.

This Policy is an English translation of the original Japanese version. In the event of any discrepancy between the two, the Japanese version shall prevail.

1. Basic Policy

The App is a sales support app for managing customers, deals, and visits on a map and sharing them within your company. Data entered in the App is stored on the user's device and in iCloud (CloudKit), provided by Apple. It is not sent to our servers, and we cannot view this data.

The App does not include any external services for advertising, usage analytics, or crash reporting. The App has no account registration or sign-in of its own; it uses the iCloud account (Apple ID) signed in on the device.

3. Storage Location and Sharing Within Your Company

App data is stored on the user's device and in the iCloud of the user who created the company (the "Owner"). It is synchronized via iCloud with the devices of users who have joined the company. The handling of information by iCloud is governed by Apple's terms and Privacy Policy.

Company data (including business data, photos, the employee list, company settings, and employee location records) is synchronized to the devices of users who have joined the company. Sharing is limited to users invited by the Owner, or devices signed in with an Apple ID shared within the company. The role and editing scope settings in the App restrict what can be viewed and edited on the App's screens.

The App does not use the iCloud public database (an area that anyone can access).

4. Location Information

Use of Current Location

With the user's permission, the App uses the device's location information for the following purposes:

  • Showing your current location on the map
  • Showing customers near your current location
  • Registering a customer at your current location

When you register a customer using your current location, that location is saved as the customer's location and shared with members of your company.

Employee Location Sharing

Only when a company administrator enables "Location Sharing" does the App record employee locations and share them with members of the company. This feature is disabled by default.

  • Each employee chooses whether their location is recorded in the App's settings. In companies where the administrator has enabled "Require location sharing for everyone," the locations of all employees are recorded.
  • While the App is open, locations are recorded at the interval set by the company (1 to 10 minutes) or when the device moves a certain distance.
  • If permitted by the company, locations are also recorded while the App is not displayed on the screen, but only while a visit is set to "On the way" or "Visiting." During this time, the device screen indicates that location information is in use.
  • Recorded locations can be viewed on the map only by employees in roles authorized by the company (a MapTech Pro subscription is required).
  • Records are deleted after the retention period set by the company (1 to 30 days) has passed, when the App is opened on the device of the employee who recorded them or of an administrator. The same applies when an employee stops sharing their location or leaves the company.
  • Employee location records are not included in webhook integration or CSV exports.

You can revoke permission to use location information at any time in the device's Settings. If you revoke permission, your current location will not be shown and your location will not be recorded.

Maps, Search, and Directions

The App uses Apple's map services to display maps, look up locations from addresses, and search for places. When you use these features, the addresses and search terms you enter and information about the displayed area are sent to Apple. When you check directions, the customer's location is passed to the Apple Maps or Google Maps app at your request.

5. Camera and Photos

The App uses the camera or photo library when a user registers a photo to a customer. From the photo library, the App reads only the photos that the user selects.

Registered photos are resized, and information such as the shooting location (metadata) is removed before they are saved. Photos are stored in the Owner's iCloud as company data and shared with members of the company.

6. On-Device AI

Suggestions for marker rules and analysis charts use the AI built into the device (Apple Intelligence). The text and data you enter are processed on the device and are not sent to us or any third party.

7. App Lock

When App Lock is enabled, your identity is verified with Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode when you open the App. Verification is performed by iOS, and the App does not receive any face or fingerprint information.

8. Webhook Integration

As a MapTech Pro feature, company administrators can configure the App to automatically send additions, changes, and deletions of customers, deals, visits, and activities to a URL specified by the administrator (HTTPS only). The data sent includes the company name, the employee number and name of the employee who made the change, and the changed data (including the customer's name, address, location, phone number, and notes, and the assigned employee's employee number and name). Photos and employee location records are not sent.

The destination is a system specified by the company, not our server. The company that specified the destination is responsible for the handling of information at the destination.

9. In-App Purchases (MapTech Pro)

Some features of the App are provided through the in-app subscription "MapTech Pro." Purchases and payments are made through Apple's App Store. We do not receive payment information such as credit card numbers. The App uses purchase information provided by the App Store to provide MapTech Pro features and restore purchases.

For information on how Apple handles purchase and payment information, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

10. Inquiry Information

When a user contacts us through our inquiry form or by email, we handle the information the user provides to the extent necessary to respond to the inquiry, such as replying or investigating issues.

Our inquiry form accepts your name, email address, phone number (optional), subject, and message, as well as the target app, inquiry type, OS you use, and device name and app version (optional).

Inquiries submitted through the form are received and managed in Salesforce, which we use. Because we cannot view App data, please do not include business data such as customer names or phone numbers in your inquiry.

Our inquiry form uses reCAPTCHA, provided by Google LLC, to prevent automated and other abusive submissions. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information about your device and browser and sends it to Google for analysis. This information is handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

11. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide information that users have provided to us to third parties, except as required by law or when outsourcing work necessary for purposes such as responding to inquiries. For situations in which Apple, Google, or Salesforce services are used, and for webhook destinations specified by the company, please refer to the preceding sections.

12. Deleting Information

  • The Owner can delete the company data in iCloud (including employee location records) and the data on the device from "Settings" → "Data" → "Reset Cloud Data" in the App. Deleted data cannot be restored.
  • The Owner can stop sharing the company or remove participants from sharing from "Settings" → "Invitations."
  • Users who have joined a company can leave it from "Settings" → "Invitations" → "Leave Company." When a user leaves, the company data is deleted from that device. Data entered by that user remains in the Owner's iCloud as company data.
  • Deleting the App from your device deletes the data stored on the device. Please delete data in iCloud using the methods above. Identifiers that the App creates for each device may remain in the device's keychain.

Because we do not store App data, we cannot delete or restore it. If you wish to request disclosure, correction, deletion, or other actions regarding inquiry information you have provided to us, please contact us using the contact information below.

13. Information About Others Entered by Users

Users can enter information such as the names, addresses, and phone numbers of their business contacts in the App. In addition, when a company enables location sharing, employee locations are recorded and shared. Users (companies) are responsible for obtaining, using, and providing such information to third parties in accordance with applicable laws. If you use employee location sharing, please inform your employees in advance of what will be recorded and for what purpose.
